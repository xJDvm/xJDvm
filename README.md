## 👋 About me

I'm a full-stack engineer based in **Valencia, Venezuela**. I like taking an idea from a blank page to something real people use, and I care as much about *what* we build and *why* as about how.

I've been **coding for 4 years** and **working with AI for 2.5**. Along the way I've worked on products for retail and healthcare, built tools for sales and support teams, and shipped side projects of my own.

> 🏆 **Winner of the Nexus Challenge (2026)**, a competition organized by Intelix and AWS Kiro to build an end-to-end sales bot, cart and checkout flow included.

## 🧭 What I bring

- **Ownership.** I like seeing a product through, from the first conversation to the day it's in people's hands.
- **Good judgment.** I weigh trade-offs, keep costs sensible, and choose the simplest thing that will hold up.
- **Teamwork.** Most of my work has been in teams, and I enjoy making the people around me faster.
- **AI in everyday work.** I use AI daily, both in the products I build and in how I work.

## 🌱 Currently

- 💼 Working as a software developer at a software consultancy
- 🤝 Open to **freelance projects** and **full-time opportunities**
- 📚 Always learning: new tools, better ways of working, and how to build useful things with AI
